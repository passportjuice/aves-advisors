Advisory across the risk and trade lifecycle.

Five practice areas, one engagement model: scoped in writing, delivered by the principal, reported in findings you can act on.

Service 01

Enterprise Risk Governance

Risk frameworks earn their keep when people can actually run them. AVES designs and reviews governance structures that give boards and management a clear view of exposure across operations, finance, and counterparties.

You may need this when: a lender or regulator questions your controls, the board asks for risk reporting it does not have, or acquisitions have left overlapping policies behind.

Engagement scope

  • Enterprise risk frameworks and risk appetite statements
  • Policy and control reviews against regulatory obligations
  • Board and management risk reporting
  • Compliance program assessment and remediation planning
  • Internal control design across finance and operations

Service 02

Forensic Audit & Investigations

When something has gone wrong, you need facts that hold up. AVES runs investigations with the discipline of an audit and the standards of evidence that counsel can use.

You may need this when: invoices do not match deliveries, expenses look inflated, a whistleblower report lands, or a dispute is heading toward court.

Engagement scope

  • Fraud and asset misappropriation investigations
  • Procurement and contract irregularity reviews
  • Employee misconduct and conflict-of-interest inquiries
  • Litigation support and expert reporting
  • Tracing flows across entities and jurisdictions

Service 03

Trade Finance Advisory

Documentary credit, guarantees, and cross-border payment structures reward precision. AVES works alongside your finance team and your banks to structure facilities and resolve problems before they turn into refused payments.

You may need this when: you are entering a new market, shipment documents have been rejected, or your bank wants security structures it does not currently offer.

Engagement scope

  • Letter of credit structuring and document review
  • Bank guarantee and standby LC arrangements
  • Discrepancy handling and refused-payment disputes
  • Counterparty and country risk review for new trade lines
  • Trade finance facility setup with banking partners

Service 04

Procurement Advisory

Procurement is where value gets committed and where leakage starts. AVES strengthens tendering, vendor selection, and contract management so decisions survive both audit and negotiation.

You may need this when: major tenders are approaching, a supplier relationship shows unusual patterns, or an audit flagged gaps in how buying happens.

Engagement scope

  • Tender and bid evaluation design
  • Vendor due diligence and red-flag screening
  • Contract lifecycle and spend controls
  • Supply chain risk assessment
  • Procurement process reviews after the fact

Service 05

Shipping, Logistics & Aviation

Cargo, charters, and aircraft move on contracts with centuries of case law behind them. AVES advises operators, traders, and financiers on the risk side of moving things.

You may need this when: a cargo claim is developing, a charter or freight contract is on the table, or a new trade lane changes your exposure.

Engagement scope

  • Charterparty, bill of lading, and freight contract review
  • Cargo loss, damage, and shortage claims support
  • Logistics provider performance and risk reviews
  • Aviation operations, leasing, and maintenance record risk
  • Trade route and corridor risk assessment
