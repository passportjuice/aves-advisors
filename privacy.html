Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy covers the website at avesadvisors.com and its GitHub Pages mirror, operated by AVES INC (AVES Strategic Advisors).

What we collect

The contact form collects only what you type into it: your name, company (optional), email address, area of interest, and the content of your message. The site does not use cookies, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or account registration.

How it is used

Information submitted through the form is used for one purpose: to read your enquiry and respond to it. It is not added to mailing lists and is not sold, shared, or used for marketing.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered through Web3Forms, a third-party form service, which processes the message content on our behalf in order to forward it to us. Their privacy policy applies to that delivery step. You can also contact us directly by email instead of using the form.

Retention

Enquiry correspondence is kept only as long as it remains relevant to the matter. You may request deletion of your correspondence at any time by emailing us.

Your rights

You may ask what personal data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, and raise complaints with your local data protection authority. Requests can be sent to contact@avesadvisors.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The date at the top of the page reflects the latest revision.
