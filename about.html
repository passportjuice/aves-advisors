About

An independent practice, built for work that has to be right.

AVES Strategic Advisors (AVES INC) exists for engagements where the findings get challenged: by a board, a regulator, a bank, or opposing counsel.

AVES Strategic Advisors serves companies that operate across borders: trading houses, shippers, financiers, and operators in aviation and logistics. The work sits where governance, money, and physical movement meet, which is exactly where problems tend to surface first.

The practice is independent by design. It takes no audit engagements and sells no products, so there is no second interest sitting behind the advice. Every engagement is led by the principal, from the first scoping call to the final report.

How engagements work

  • Scoped in writing. You know what is being examined, over what period, and what the deliverable is before work starts.
  • Direct access. You work with the person doing the work. There is no team to be handed off to.
  • Confidential by default. Findings are shared only with the people you choose.
  • Findings in writing. Reports state what was examined, what was found, and what we recommend, in language a board can read.
